E5

Methods and limitations

How to read the enforcement story

Source

BITRE Road Safety Enforcement Data, 2008–2024. Detailed age, location and detection records are available from 2023.

Processing

Historic aggregate rows and 2023–24 detailed rows are processed separately. The reproducible script and original CSV files are included in the repository.

Comparability

Definitions, enforcement programs and public reporting vary across jurisdictions and years. A zero can mean no recorded value, not necessarily no activity.

Interpretation

Fine counts describe detected and recorded events. They should not be treated as offence prevalence, personal risk or evidence that one jurisdiction is inherently safer.
