Interpretation limit: all values are recorded counts. They do not measure population risk, crash exposure, severity for an individual, or the quality of hospital care.

A

Hospitalisations

Recorded hospital admissions following road crashes. Values are absolute counts and are not population-adjusted rates.

B

Bed-days

Total occupied hospital days for included cases. The KNIME workflow subtracts bed-days associated with people who died in hospital, following the published definition.

C

Road users

The injured person’s road-user role, including car driver, passenger, motorcyclist, pedal cyclist and pedestrian.

D

First Nations profile share

How each status group’s recorded total is distributed across age groups. It is not a comparison of population risk.

E

Missing values

Published “n.p.” values remain missing. They are excluded from totals and are never changed to zero.

F

Data preparation

KNIME cleaned labels, checked values and duplicates, created dates and sorting fields, and exported five validated analytical views.
